- **Return Window**: 30 days from delivery date for most items.

- **Refund Processing**: Refunds processed within 5–7 business days after receiving the return.
- **Non-Returnable Items**: Gift cards, clearance items marked "Final Sale", and personalized products.

- **Smart Home Devices**: Covered under manufacturer warranty; Philips Hue, Ring, and TP-Link offer dedicated support portals.
- **Warranty Claims**: Contact TechHaven support with order number and issue description to initiate a warranty claim.

## Loyalty Program — TechHaven Rewards

# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?
You can track your order by logging into your TechHaven account and visiting the "My Orders" section. You will also receive an email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships. Our carriers include UPS, FedEx, and USPS.

## Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Contact support immediately if you need to make changes.

## Do you offer price matching?
Yes! TechHaven offers price matching within 14 days of purchase. If you find a lower price at an authorized retailer (Amazon, Best Buy, Target), contact support with the product link and we will match the price or issue the difference as store credit.

## How do I use my loyalty points?
Your loyalty points are automatically displayed at checkout. Select "Apply Rewards Points" to redeem them. 100 points equals $1 in savings. Gold and Platinum members earn bonus points on every purchase.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and TechHaven Gift Cards. Affirm financing (buy now, pay later) is available on orders over $100.

## Do you ship internationally?
Currently, TechHaven ships within the United States and Canada. International shipping is expected to launch in Q1 2027.
